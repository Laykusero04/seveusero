MEETRADIUS

Local meetups, not endless feeds. — discover, join, and meet nearby within your radius.

ANDROID IOS WEB

OVERVIEW

MeetRadius is a location-first social app for hosting and joining nearby real-world activities. Users find meetups within a configurable discovery radius (default 15 miles), join groups, coordinate in chat, check in on arrival, and share moments — anchored to local discovery rather than a global endless feed.

TECH_STACK

Flutter Dart BLoC / Cubit Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Cloud Functions Firebase Storage FCM Push Google Maps go_router

CORE_FEATURES

FEED

Browse live and upcoming activities near you — filter by category and phase, with Near, Following, and For You tabs.

MAP

View activities on a map within your discovery radius — see what's happening around you at a glance.

ACTIVITIES

Host meetups with time, place, category, and capacity — or join, edit, end, or leave existing ones.

CHAT

Group chat per activity for coordination, plus direct messages between users.

CHECK-IN

Check in at the meeting spot on arrival — weekly streak tracked on your profile.

MOMENTS

Share photo moments tied to activities and build a lightweight social graph.

PLATFORM

Location & Discovery

  • Discovery anchor via GPS or manual city/ZIP
  • ~15 mile radius for feed and map results
  • Follow users and see activities outside your radius

Social & Profile

  • Follow users, invite friends, and see connections
  • Profile with display name, photo, gallery, and streak badge
  • Push notifications with deep links into activities and chats

Auth & Navigation

  • Email/password and Google Sign-In with verification
  • Four tabs: Feed, Map, Chats, and Menu
  • Device session management and app_links deep linking

Safety & Settings

  • Block users, report content, and blocked users list
  • GPS vs manual location, theme preferences
  • Account center, terms/privacy, and in-app help/FAQ
