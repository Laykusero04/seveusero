CARTOON_EVERYTHING

Turn your imagination into cartoon art — AI-powered image and video generation with multiple styles and themes.

ANDROID IOS

OVERVIEW

Cartoon Everything is an AI-powered creative app that transforms photos and videos into stunning cartoon-style artwork. Users pick from popular themes and trending styles, generate results with a credit-based system, and fine-tune outputs — all through a fast, mobile-first experience built on third-party AI APIs.

TECH_STACK

Flutter Dart Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Firebase Storage Third-Party AI APIs

CORE_FEATURES

IMAGE TO CARTOON

Upload a photo and transform it into cartoon art using AI — quick actions from the home screen for instant creation.

VIDEO TO CARTOON

Bring videos to life in cartoon style with the same streamlined generate-and-save workflow.

THEMES & STYLES

Browse popular themes like 3D Cartoon and Collectible Figures, plus trending styles such as Manga B&W and Cinematic Fire Set.

STYLE DETAIL

Preview each style with ratings and usage counts, then generate or open advanced fine-tune options before creating.

CREDITS

Credit-based generation system shown across the app — balance displayed on home and per-style detail screens.

HISTORY

Five-tab navigation — Home, Themes, Create, History, and More — for browsing, generating, and revisiting past work.

HIGHLIGHTS

AI Integration

  • Third-party API integration for image generation
  • Multiple artistic styles and theme presets
  • Advanced fine-tune options per style

User Experience

  • Dark-mode UI with quick-action shortcuts
  • Trending and popular theme discovery
  • Fast create, save, and share workflow
