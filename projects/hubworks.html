HUB_WORKS

Hire. Work. Grow. — a freelance marketplace connecting clients and skilled professionals worldwide.

ANDROID IOS

OVERVIEW

Hub Works is a freelance marketplace where clients post projects and professionals bid, collaborate, and get paid securely. The platform covers the full workflow — from job discovery and bidding to real-time messaging and milestone-based payments with payment protection built into the chat experience.

TECH_STACK

Flutter Dart Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Stripe Real-Time Chat

CORE_FEATURES

JOB SEARCH

Discover projects by skill and category — Web Dev, Design, Writing, Marketing — with budget ranges and bid counts on each listing.

PROJECT BIDDING

Freelancers browse open posts and submit bids; clients review proposals and select talent for their projects.

SELLER DASHBOARD

Performance hub with monthly stats, order completions, ratings, impressions graph, and quick links to jobs, messages, and contracts.

MESSAGING

Real-time chat between clients and freelancers with project context, online status, and attachment support.

SECURE PAYMENTS

Milestone payments with payment protection — escrow-style cards shown directly in the conversation flow.

VERIFIED TALENT

Verified professional badges and global opportunities for clients and freelancers on one platform.

HIGHLIGHTS

For Freelancers

  • Find jobs with category filters and search
  • Track bids, contracts, and attendance
  • Seller hub with impressions and ratings

For Clients

  • Post projects and receive competitive bids
  • Chat and manage milestones in one place
  • Payment-protected transactions via Stripe
