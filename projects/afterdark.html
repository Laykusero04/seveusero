AFTERDARK

Dating + social + community — swipe to connect, share on a feed, chat, discover nearby, and organize through groups & events.

ANDROID IOS WEB

OVERVIEW

AfterDark is a society-focused social and dating app built around five main tabs: Meet, Nearby, Feed, Messages, and Society. Users build a profile, verify their age, set interests, and explore connections through swiping, map-based discovery, a social timeline, real-time chat, and community groups & events — with age verification, moderation, and subscription options built in.

TECH_STACK

Flutter Dart Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Firebase Storage FCM Push Google Maps In-App Purchases Stripe i18n (EN / ES)

CORE_EXPERIENCE

MEET

Swipe deck with pass, like, and spark. Match celebrations, filters, daily limits, rewind passes, pre-chat safety gate, and matches list.

NEARBY

Google Maps discovery with manual check-in, marker previews, list view, visibility controls, and required safety consent before first use.

FEED

For You, Latest, and Following feeds. Posts with polls, comments, reactions, bookmarks, tags, trending, and group content mixed in.

STORIES

Create image/text stories with editors, full-screen playback, close friends, daily limits, highlights, and share-post-to-story.

MESSAGES

1:1 and group chat with GIFs, pinned messages, typing indicators, likes inbox, message requests, screenshot detection, and spark-gated DMs.

SOCIETY

Groups with feeds, chat, sub-groups, join screening, and itinerary tools. Events with RSVP, discovery, and calendar integration.
