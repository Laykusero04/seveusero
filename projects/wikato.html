WIKATO

Learn. Speak. Connect. — your journey to Philippine languages and culture starts here.

ANDROID IOS

OVERVIEW

Wikato is a language-learning platform focused on Philippine languages and culture. Users master words, phrases, and expressions through engaging lessons designed for real conversations — with interactive modules, pronunciation practice, vocabulary building, and cultural context woven into every learning path.

TECH_STACK

Flutter Dart Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Audio Playback

CORE_FEATURES

DASHBOARD

Personalized home with streaks, lesson progress, and badges — card-based modules for greetings, vocabulary, reading, and culture.

FLASHCARDS

Word cards with native terms, English translations, and context — tap to pronounce and reinforce retention.

PRONUNCIATION

Listen-and-repeat practice with tap-to-hear phrases like Kamusta, Salamat po, and Magandang araw.

VOCABULARY BUILDER

Dedicated module to grow word banks with structured new-word tracking per lesson.

READ & UNDERSTAND

Phrase-in-context lessons that move beyond single words into real conversational usage.

CULTURA

Heritage and cultural exploration alongside language — preserving languages and celebrating culture.

HIGHLIGHTS

Learning Design

  • Beginner to advanced — learn anytime, anywhere
  • Gamification with streaks, lessons, and badges
  • Structured pages with progress tracking (e.g. 5/10 lessons)

Philippine Focus

  • Regional Philippine language support
  • Pronunciation guides with audio playback
  • Cultural context integrated into lessons
