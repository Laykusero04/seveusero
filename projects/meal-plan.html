MEAL_PLAN

Plan meals. Live better. Together. — organize daily, weekly, and monthly meals and share plans in real time.

ANDROID IOS

OVERVIEW

Meal Plan is a collaborative meal planning app for organizing breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks across daily, weekly, and monthly views. Families and friends can plan together with real-time sync, auto-generated grocery lists, and recipe inspiration — built for families planning together.

TECH_STACK

Flutter Dart Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Real-Time Sync

CORE_FEATURES

HOME

Today's meals at a glance with quick actions to add plans or meals — breakfast through snacks in one daily view.

PLANS

Week and month calendar views to organize meals ahead — tap any day to see or edit that day's plan.

DISHES

Recipe and dish library to discover ideas and build better eating habits for every meal.

GROCERY LIST

Auto-generated shopping lists from your meal plan with progress tracking, pantry management, and today's ingredients.

COLLABORATION

Invite family and friends to plan together — changes sync instantly across all devices.

NOTIFICATIONS

Stay organized every day with reminders so you never miss a planned meal.
