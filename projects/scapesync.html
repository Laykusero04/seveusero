SCAPESYNC

From quote to payment. — landscaping & field service management for owners, office staff, and field teams.

WEB

OVERVIEW

ScapeSync is a web-only platform for running a landscaping business from first quote through job completion and payment. Owners, office staff, and field crews share one workspace for customers, estimates, scheduled jobs, invoices, maps, weather, and day-to-day operations — live at scapesync.org.

TECH_STACK

React Next.js Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Maps Scheduling

CORE_FEATURES

CUSTOMERS

Keep property and client records in one place so estimates, jobs, and invoices stay tied to the right account.

ESTIMATES

Build quotes from services and send them to customers — the start of the quote-to-payment workflow.

JOBS & CALENDAR

Schedule one-off and recurring work, assign crews, and see the day’s jobs on a shared calendar.

MAP & WEATHER

Field-aware operations with job locations on the map and weather context for outdoor crews.

INVOICES

Turn completed work into invoices so billing follows the job instead of living in a separate spreadsheet.

OPS TOOLS

Timesheets, inventory, mileage, expenses, reports, and team settings for owners running the full business.

HIGHLIGHTS

For Owners & Office

  • Company setup checklist and staff tools
  • Customers, services, estimates, and invoices
  • Reports, team settings, and company info

For Field Teams

  • Jobs, calendar, and recurring routes
  • Map, weather, timesheets, and mileage
  • Inventory and expenses from the same workspace
